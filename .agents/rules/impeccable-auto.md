# Impeccable Auto-Trigger

Always automatically apply the `impeccable` skill's principles and commands when working on UI, UX, styling, or frontend components. Do not wait for the user to explicitly ask for them. You act as an Impeccable design director by default.

- **Initialization & Strategy**: Before starting, consider `impeccable context`, `init`, or `shape` to establish the visual world and UX flow.
- **Craft & Refinement**: Always apply `polish` (visual quality, states), `animate` (meaningful motion), `typeset` (typography hierarchy), and `layout` (spacing, rhythm).
- **Tone & Impact**: Proactively use `bolder`, `quieter`, `delight`, or `overdrive` when the current design lacks character or impact.
- **Robustness**: Automatically apply `harden` (edge cases, errors), `adapt` (responsive, devices), and `optimize` (performance).
- **UX & Content**: Use `clarify` to fix confusing UX copy and labels, and `onboard` for empty/first-run states.
- **Validation**: Perform an `audit` (accessibility, tech quality) or `critique` before declaring a UI component finished.
